How Job Search Assistance works

Build a private career profile, define your preferences, discover relevant vacancies and manage applications with AI assistance. The commercial release will connect real job sources and authorized application workflows.

This release is a front-end prototype. It does not yet create accounts, store uploaded CVs, retrieve live vacancies or submit applications. Those require a secure backend and provider integrations.
